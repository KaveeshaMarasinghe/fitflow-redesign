# FitFlow photos

Eight original AI-generated photographs created with the built-in image generation tool for this prototype. The people and activities are illustrative sample content. These are app artwork, not evidence of actual community activity or device testing.

`welcome.jpg`, `strength.jpg`, and `running.jpg` are used for fitness banners and community cards. `alex.jpg` is the fictional sample profile portrait. `oats.jpg`, `chicken.jpg`, `yogurt.jpg`, and `salmon.jpg` match the sample meal names.

Images are encoded as optimized JPEGs (1024 pixels wide for hero images; 512 pixels wide for food and portrait images), bundled through static sources in `data/photos.ts`, and require no remote image service. Full generation prompts are in [PROMPTS.json](PROMPTS.json).
